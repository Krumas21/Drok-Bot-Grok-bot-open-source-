## When to use
Any question that needs current facts from the internet.

## Steps
1. Restate the question and what a good answer contains.
2. Search broadly, then open the 3-6 most authoritative results (primary sources first).
3. Extract facts with their URLs. Cross-check key claims against a second source.
4. Write /workspace/research/<slug>.md: Summary (<=5 bullets), Findings (with citations), Unknowns.

## Validate
Every claim has a URL. Conflicting sources are called out, not silently resolved.

## Return
The summary bullets and the file path.

## Needs owner approval
Nothing unless the research requires signing up, paying, or contacting someone.
